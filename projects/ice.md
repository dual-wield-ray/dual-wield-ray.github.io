### The project

Infocomm is a trade show that brings together companies accross the multimedia landscape. For the Moment Factory booth, we created a customizable showcase experience. The wall featured three interactive capsules, that could be controlled by a web app from an operator tablet.

### The role

I was the single Unity developer on the butterfly capsule. I handled all programming, asset integration, visual improvements, and webapp connectivity. The Unity app offered different presets for color palettes, butterfly amounts and sizes, and flocking behaviour, which reacted to comamnds from the operator tablet.
