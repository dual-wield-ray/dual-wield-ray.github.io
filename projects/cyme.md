### The project

I joined the Eaton office in Brossard to bring my expertise in software quality and project management to the CYME software solution. This battle-tested software is used by electrical engineers all over the world to simulate electrical networks under real-world circumstances.

### The role

My mandate on this project is to promote the continuous improvement of our technologies suite by bringing my creative background to the table. Through code refactoring, API refinement, Dev Ops management, quality assurance, workflow trainings, and user experience improvements, I work hard to bring the software stack into the modern era. I'm also the main point of contact for software packaging, deployment, and release orchestration.
