<h3>The Project</h3>
<p>
Showcasing the beauty of the Carrefour Laval Mall's central gardens, this interactive wall featured two seasonal activations. During the fall, butterflies fly around ready to marvel visitors when they touch the surface. Standing still allows the butterflies land, making for a great photo opportunity.
</p>

<p>During the winter, it turned into a frosty surface evoking a winter wonderland, with multiple color palettes that would change during the course of a day.</p>

<h3>Contribution</h3>
<p>I was the lead developer on the fall exhibit, in charge of gameplay programming, integration, quality assurance, and deployment onsite.</p>
