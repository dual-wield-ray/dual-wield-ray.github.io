### The project

Showcasing the beauty of the Carrefour Laval Mall's central gardens, this interactive wall featured two seasonal activations. During the fall, butterflies fly around ready to marvel visitors when they touch the surface. Standing still allows the butterflies land to, making for a great photo opportunity.

### The role

I was the lead developer on the fall exhibit, in charge of gameplay programming, quality assurance, and deployment onsite. This short and sweet project required fast integration of new assets and attention to detail to obtain an elegant interactive wall that would entertain mall visitors (especially children!).
