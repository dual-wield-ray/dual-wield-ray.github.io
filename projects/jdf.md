 <h3>The Project</h3>
<p>At the Pasino Grand in Aix-en-Provence, visitors can experience the Jeux du Futur, a series of interactive games on massive screens played with a host in front of an audience. Over a long partnership with Moment Factory, the Jeux du Futur contains a big library of games on offer.</p>

<h3>Contribution</h3>
<p>I was responsible for working on the last two games in the Jeux du Futur: Héros du Casino, and Fast Draw. The first is an action game where two players duel to grab the right suits as they fall from the top of the screen, while the latter challenges the player to draw the shapes shown using wall like a touch screen. In both cases, I was the main programmer and handled gameplay programmin, asset integration, and quality assurance.</p>
