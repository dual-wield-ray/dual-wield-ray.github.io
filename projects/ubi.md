### The project

As a first foray into the software industry, I was brought on the animation programming department of the Ubisoft Montreal studio. First working on an unannounced game, then later as part of the Technology Group, I worked on a cross-engine, runtime IK solution aimed at technical animators. Our libraries and tools helped the various creatures of Ubisoft's worlds come to life.

### The role

I helped a team of senior animation programmers with their tasks, particularly with regards to developing the tools inside the Autodesk suite of software: Maya, Motionbuilder and 3ds Max. I also worked on a cross-platform API for animation programming in those environments, created documentation and video presentations, and fixed bugs as they came. Later, I assisted with the integration of our IK technologies in the multiple in-house engines at the studio, namely Anvil and Snowdrop.

While this was my junior job out of University, I learned a lot by working at Ubisoft. While I did not specialize in animation programming in the end, I started building the skillset that would later define my career; tools programming, API design, code quality, and technical project management.
