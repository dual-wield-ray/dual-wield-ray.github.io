### The project

At the Pasino Grand in Aix-en-Provence, visitors can experience the Jeux du Futur, a series of interactive games on massive screens played with a host in front of an audience. Over a long partnership with Moment Factory, the Jeux du Futur offers a sizeable collection of games that are played in front of an audience and energetic host.

One of the defining features of the Jeux du Futur is the precise touchscreen technology at its center, which allows for game designs not often found in games at this scale.

### The role

I was responsible for working on the latest two games in the Jeux du Futur library: Héros du Casino and Fast Draw. The first is an action game where two players duel to grab the right suits as they fall from the top of the screen, while the latter challenges the player to draw the shapes shown using wall like a touch screen. Héros du Casino in particular received great feedback from audiences and quickly became a very popular choice at the JDF. For both games, I was the main programmer and handled gameplay programming, asset integration, and quality assurance.
